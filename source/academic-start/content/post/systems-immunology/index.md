## Systems-Level Molecular Immunology

This digital textbook provides a rigorous yet intuitive conceptual foundation in immunology, translating complex biological cascades into systems-level models, logic gates, and pattern-recognition principles:

- **Algorithmic & Mechanistic Framing:** Biological pathways are framed through signal integration, Boolean checkpoints (e.g., Signal 1 + Signal 2 activation), stochastic recombination kinetics ($V(D)J$ rearrangement), and evolutionary trade-offs.
- **Multimodal Visual Showcases:** Key immunological events feature interactive showcases including vector SVGs, systems flowcharts, and 3D molecular structures.
- **Accessible Conceptual Hierarchy:** Each chapter opens with an executive overview summarizing core mechanisms, followed by deep mechanistic explanations, gene cards, and clinical case logic.
- **Interactive Tooling:** Full LaTeX/MathJax equation formatting, built-in dual dark/light theme switching, and an inline terminology glossary.
